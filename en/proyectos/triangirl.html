The challenge

Create a brand from scratch for Laboratorios Ordesa: natural, scientifically tested products so girls can live their lives without their period setting the pace.

The name

TriangleTriangirl

The triangle has always been a shape linked to women. And the sound of “triangle” let us slip our audience right into the name: girl. That’s how Triangirl was born, with the triangle’s V as its symbol.

The claim

Until now, they were the ones

following the rules

of their body.

From now on, #MisReglas.

They change the rules and decide what to do at every moment. #MisReglas means #MyRules: a claim built to empower, said naturally and without drama.

Building the brand

A simple, recognisable system: a V that signs every piece, a palette that sets each product apart and a voice that speaks plainly.

01 · Logo

A triangle that opens into a V.

We started from the triangle, the shape that gives the brand its name, and took away its base: that is how the V that signs every piece was born. Above it sits the name, in capitals and generously spaced, at the same width as the symbol so the two work as a single block.

  1. 1The V is a triangle open at the top.
  2. 2Name and symbol share the same width.
  3. 3The gap between them, x, also sets the clear space.

02 · Iconography

Each product, its own icons.

A family of fine-line icons drawn on the same grid. At a glance they tell you what each product does and what it is made of, and each one takes the colour of its pack.
